**Subject: Adding a second step to your district sign-in**

Hi everyone,

Starting [DATE], `<DISTRICT_NAME>` accounts will use 2-Step Verification — a second quick
check when you sign in.

**Why now.** Attackers are actively targeting school districts, and a stolen password alone
is enough to reach payroll, student records, and staff email. A second step stops that.

**What you need to do**

1. Go to [link] and set it up — about **three minutes**
2. Choose your method: phone prompt, an app, or a security key
3. Do it before **[ENFORCEMENT DATE]**

After [ENFORCEMENT DATE] you'll need it to sign in.

**Help is available**
- Drop-in sessions: [times and locations]
- Help desk: `<SECURITY_ALIAS>`
- Step-by-step guide: [link]

**If you work in payroll, HR, finance, or IT,** you'll get a security key instead. We'll
reach out individually — those roles are targeted specifically, and a key is the only method
that stops the current attacks.

**No phone? Don't want to use your personal phone?** Contact us. We have options and nobody
is required to use a personal device.
